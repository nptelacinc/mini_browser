⚙ Connection Procedure

  1. Main Supply OFF karo.
  2. MCB OFF position me rakho.
  3. Phase wire motor terminal me connect karo.
  4. Neutral wire connect karo.
  5. Earthing motor body se connect karo.
  6. Sabhi screw terminals tight karo.
  7. Insulation check karo.
  8. MCB ON karo.
  9. Motor run karke test karo.

⚠ Safety Precautions

  • Supply OFF karke hi wiring karo.
  • Earthing zarur connect karo.
  • Loose wire use mat karo.
  • Wet hands se wiring mat karo.
  • Proper MCB use karo.
